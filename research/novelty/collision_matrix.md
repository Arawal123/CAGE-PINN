# CAGE-PINN collision matrix

Status: provisional screen through 2026-07-24. Levels reflect the material in
`research/literature/`; they are not a claim of exhaustive priority.

The essential combination under test is:

> Ground-truth-free, online, cross-fitted audit of unseen physics residual risk
> that drives compute-cost-aware allocation among complementary residual-
> enforcement operators, with an untouched monitor fold and plug-in use across
> PINN backbones.

## Collision questions

No seed-screened source verified all of the following in one method:

1. learner and rotating selector/monitor/calibration physics samples;
2. selector residual risk influences allocation but never a direct update;
3. monitor influences neither allocation nor parameter gradient;
4. tail-sensitive bounded off-grid residual risk and a train-audit gap;
5. gradient-predicted utility per measured compute;
6. allocation across strong, residual-slope, and weak enforcement forms; and
7. unchanged use across vanilla, XPINN, and adaptive-basis parents.

Absence from this seed screen is not proof of absence from the literature.
